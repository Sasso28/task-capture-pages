Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who runs this service

Task Capture Bot is a personal project run by its owner for their own use. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to other people.

What the bot processes

  • Messages you send: text messages and voice notes sent to the bot on Telegram. Only the owner's Telegram account is allowed to use the bot; messages from any other account are ignored.
  • Voice notes: sent to OpenAI for transcription. The audio is discarded after transcription and is not kept by the bot.
  • Transcribed text: sent to Anthropic's API, which extracts the action, priority, due date and category.
  • Pending questions: when the bot needs more detail, it stores the open question and your partial answers in a Cloudflare D1 database. Unanswered questions expire after 24 hours.

Where your data is stored

  • Google Drive: each item is saved as a Markdown file in a category folder (Ideas, To do, Things to study) in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Google Calendar: reminders are created as calendar events in the owner's calendar.
  • Cloudflare D1: the pending-question state described above, plus the bot's internal records needed to ignore duplicate Telegram updates.

Google data

The bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The bot accesses Google Drive and Google Calendar only to create, edit and delete items that the owner requests. Google data is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone other than the service providers listed on this page.

Third-party services

Data is processed by Telegram, Cloudflare, Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. Each handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Deleting data

Items can be deleted through the bot, which moves the Drive file to the Drive trash and cancels the calendar event. The owner can also remove files and events directly in Google Drive and Google Calendar.
