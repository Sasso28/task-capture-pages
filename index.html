Task Capture Bot

Task Capture Bot is a personal Telegram bot. It turns text or voice messages, in English or Spanish, into organised notes for tasks, ideas and study items. Each item is saved as a Markdown file in Google Drive, and reminders are added to Google Calendar.

The bot is for private use by its owner only. It is not offered to the public.

See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
